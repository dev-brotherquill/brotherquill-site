You picked up your phone to check one thing.

Forty minutes later you were still holding it. Most of us know that feeling, and most of us have tried an app or two to fix it.

Brother Quill is a gentler kind of timer. It gives your time somewhere to go.

How it works

  1. One

    Pick a length

    When you need to focus, open Brother Quill and choose 15, 25, 45, or 60 minutes.

  2. Two

    Get to work

    You focus on your work, and your focus time adds up, verse by verse, at about 68 characters a minute. That’s roughly as fast as a person copies by hand. The verse numbers go in red.

  3. Three

    Come back next time

    When your time is up, or you stop the timer yourself, the copying stops. The verses stay in your Bible, and next time you pick up right where you left off.

“Three fingers write, but the whole body labors.”

A line medieval scribes often wrote at the end of a manuscript

Before printing, every Bible was copied this way, one page at a time, for well over a thousand years. Brother Quill is a small nod to that work. He writes in a manuscript hand, or in plain type if you’d rather, and he doesn’t hurry.

Why I made Brother Quill

Most focus apps count your time in hours: 100 hours, 200 hours. I wanted to see mine as Scripture.

The app does the copying. Still, as a believer, watching my work hours turn into verses and chapters helps me stay with what I’m doing.

I also think reading a Bible copied this way, a few verses after each session, means something a little different from opening the same pages anywhere else.

A few things you should know

  • Brother Quill is a focus timer. It shows your focus time as Scripture copied, and you can read what he’s copied whenever you like.
