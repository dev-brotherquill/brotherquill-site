It’s hard to stay with one thing for long.

A paper due Monday. A report you’ve opened three times today. You know what to do next, and your hand still goes to your phone.

It’s easier to keep your mind on your work when someone else is working too. That’s why some people get more done at the office than at home, and why some put on study-with-me videos.

Brother Quill keeps you company the same way. While you work, he copies Scripture on your phone.

How it works

  1. One

    Choose a book and a length

    The first time, pick a Bible and the book he starts with. After that, choose 15, 25, 45, or 60 minutes each time.

  2. Two

    Get to work

    Stand your phone up next to your work, where you can see him writing. Leave it there until the timer ends. Every minute you work, he copies about a dozen words. If you switch apps, the session keeps going, and your Lock Screen shows the time left and the verse he’s on.

  3. Three

    Take a break

    When time’s up, or when you stop the timer, he stops too. The verses he copied stay in your Bible, and next session he picks up where he left off.

Say you spend two hours on a report and get it done. That night, open your Bible and read the verses he copied while you wrote it.

Why I made Brother Quill

I focus best when the work is something I enjoy thinking about, or when there’s a deadline I can’t miss.

The room also has to be quiet. No music, no white noise. And someone else focused on their own work, like the one person at a distant table in a nearly empty library, helps just by being there. I lean on them without noticing.

Brother Quill is meant to be that person at the distant table.

While I work, he copies Scripture. Knowing that, I work with a little more reverence. It feels like being held close to God, and that helps me stay with it.

Why a monk

Before printing, Bibles were copied by hand, one page at a time, for more than a thousand years. In many monasteries, the monks did it in one room, each at his own desk, working in silence. At the end of a manuscript, scribes often left a line like this one:

“Three fingers write, but the whole body labors.”

A few things you should know

  • It’s a focus timer first. You can read what he’s copied any time, in the app.
  • No ads, no accounts. Your Bibles are stored on your iPhone, not on our servers, and your iPhone backup includes them.
  • It doesn’t block other apps yet.
  • He writes in a manuscript style, or in plain type if you prefer.
  • He writes without a sound. When the time is up, you get one notification.
