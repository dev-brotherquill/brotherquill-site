Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Brother Quill is made by Strength Theory, LLC. This policy covers the Brother Quill iPhone app and this website, brotherquill.com. The short version: the app keeps your sessions and your Bibles on your iPhone, and we don't collect them.

The app

  • Your Bibles and sessions stay on your device. Your progress, settings, and session history are stored only on your iPhone. We don't run servers that receive them.
  • No accounts, no tracking. The app has no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Notifications that tell you a session has ended are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active.

This website

  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address and answers you give in the form (translation, tradition, language, and how you heard about Brother Quill). If you came to the site through a link on one of our own channels, such as our Instagram profile, the form also records which channel the link was on. The form is run by Tally, which processes your answers for us. Because the form is embedded on the page, your browser loads it from Tally when you open the page, which sends Tally your IP address and basic browser information, and Tally may store its own cookies or similar data. See Tally’s privacy policy. We use your email only to tell you about Brother Quill, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Email. If you write to us, we receive your name, email address, and whatever you include in your message. Our email is hosted by Zoho. See Zoho’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and may record browser, device, and page information, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • No analytics or advertising. We don't set cookies or use analytics or advertising on this site. (The embedded Tally form may set its own, as described above.) We don't respond to Do Not Track signals, because we don't track.

How we use and share information

We use the information above to send you Brother Quill news you asked for, to answer you, to keep the site running and secure, and to follow the law. We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers named on this page, when the law requires it, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as we need it for these purposes or as the law requires. If you unsubscribe from the waitlist, we stop emailing you. This site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections, and we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold, though no method is perfectly secure.

International visitors

We are based in the United States. Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries, whose laws may differ from yours.

Children

Brother Quill is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete information you gave us by emailing us. We may need to confirm your identity first. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local law.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
